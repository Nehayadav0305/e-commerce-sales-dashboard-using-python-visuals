# e-commerce-sales-dashboard-using-python-visuals
Developed an interactive E-Commerce Sales Dashboard to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, and product category trends. The dashboard combines Power BI for business intelligence reporting and Python visualizations for custom analytics, enabling data-driven decision-making through clear KPI tracking and visual insights.

#  Key Features
Designed interactive slicers for Product, Product Category, and Device Type filtering.

Built KPI cards to monitor:
Total Sales
Total Profit
Total Quantity Sold

Created Python-powered visualizations for:
Sales by Gender
Profit by Gender
Sales by Product Category

Implemented dynamic filtering to explore performance across different customer and product segments.
Applied data aggregation and transformation techniques for meaningful business insights.

# Tools & Technologies
Power BI
Python
Pandas
Matplotlib
Data Visualization
Business Intelligence

# Business Insights Generated
Identified revenue contribution by customer gender.
Analyzed profit distribution across customer segments.
Evaluated product category performance and sales trends.
Compared customer purchasing behavior across different device types.
